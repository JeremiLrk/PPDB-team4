# Club Home Page

The club home page is the starting point for a specific club.

## What It Shows

The club page shows:

- the club name
- the club description
- the ladders inside the club
- the top players for those ladders
- a short club summary with the current competition context
- upcoming matches
- latest results

## What You Can Do From There

When the user has permission, the club page also gives access to club settings.

It also acts as the launch point for the club's ladder pages.

The page also shows a clear call to action for:

- club managers to go into settings
- logged-out users to register or log in

## Layout

The page is arranged as a three-part summary view:

- ladders on one side
- club summary in the middle
- top performers plus match summaries around it

Upcoming matches and latest results are shown in compact batches so the page stays readable on desktop and mobile.
