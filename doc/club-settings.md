# Club Settings

Club settings are the management area for one club.

## Main Sections

The settings area is split into separate sections so club management stays organized:

- overview
- general club information
- ladders
- members
- owners
- match rules and rulesets
- disputed matches
- back to club home
- sidebar navigation

## What It Is For

This is where a club owner or other authorized user handles the club’s structure and rules.

The settings pages are where you:

- review a club summary
- edit club details
- manage ladders
- create and delete rulesets for ladders
- manage members and owners
- review disputed matches
- go back to the club home page

## Rulesets

Rulesets live in the match-rules section of club settings.

That page is where club staff can:

- create a new ruleset
- inspect the rules that a ladder will use
- delete a ruleset they own
